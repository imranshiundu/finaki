Est. 2022 — Nairobi / Melbourne

Refuse to be underestimated.

FINAKI is an independent design studio founded by Imran Shiundu in Nairobi. We build change-making websites and brand experiences for founder-led brands whose presence hasn't caught up to what they've built.

01

Why we exist

( The gap )
15+

Founder-led brands — from disruptive creative agencies to consumer brands.

Great founders changing the world deserve a presence as powerful as what they're building. Most founders we work with are building something significant, but their presence doesn't show it yet.

That gap costs more than revenue. It costs the certainty that your brand is finally being understood. So we close it — strategy first, then design that proves it, then a website that sells it while you sleep.

02

Our story

( 2022 — now )

FINAKI started in Nairobi with one observation: the most ambitious founders were being undersold by their own websites.

Not because they lacked vision — because the people building their presence never asked for it. We do the opposite. Every project starts with your story, not our portfolio. We dig until we find what makes you irreplaceable, then we design the proof.

Four years in, that approach has taken us from a Nairobi one-room studio to working with brands across three continents — and it hasn't changed once.

Imran Shiundu Founder, FINAKI
03

How we work

( 04 Values )
01

Listen

Every engagement starts with your story — dug out properly, not skimmed from a brief. We don't design until we understand.

02

Strategy first, then design that proves it. Identity, digital design and front-end execution treated as one system.

03

The last 5% is where premium lives. Motion, type, rhythm — we obsess over the details most people feel but never notice.

04

Inspire

A presence should do more than look good — it should make your team proud and your clients certain.
