CASE 01

TapTap Africa

Nairobi — Cashless payments

Cashless payments for live events: NFC wristbands and QR codes on M-Pesa rails, with wallets, refunds and vendor settlement in one platform.

The brand, the marketing site and the product surface were designed and engineered in-house; the platform is operated by the TapTap team inside Olarfe Africa.

Private Nairobi tours and JKIA layover safaris, quoted in writing and settled on the day. The coordination runs under Inkind Experiences.

Explore Kibera Tours

Nairobi — Community tourism

Community-led walking tours of Kibera, resident-guided, with 100% of tour fees reinvested in the community projects the walks visit.

The booking site and coordination tools were designed and engineered in-house, under Inkind Experiences.
